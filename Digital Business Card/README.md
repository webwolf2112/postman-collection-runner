# Digital Business Card

A standalone tutorial (not part of the Postman Collections and Variables series) on how to use Claude to build a digital business card page — one with a "Save Contact" button that saves your info straight to someone's phone as a vCard, plus a QR code linking to the page.

## What's here

- [`digital-business-card-prompt.md`](digital-business-card-prompt.md) — the exact, reusable prompt used in the video. Fill in your own details in the `[BRACKETS]`, paste it into Claude along with a photo, and answer the follow-up questions it asks you.
